A home away from home since June 28, 1993.

Aladdin's Castle Learning Center is owned by Mrs. April C. Terrell. Every weekday, Odessa families hand their little ones to warm, experienced teachers in clean, ready classrooms.

  • Experienced staff. Warm, consistent teachers who know each stage of growth.
  • Clean facilities. A welcoming center kept tidy for play, learning, and rest.
  • Strong communication. Parents as partners, with an open door for questions.
