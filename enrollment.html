Enrollment

We're glad you're considering Aladdin's Castle. Here's what to expect when you enroll — and how to get started with a tour or packet.

Call to schedule a tour Send a message

Enrollment requirements

Before your child's first day, the following must be complete:

  1. Enrollment & medical forms

    All enrollment and medical forms must be completed and signed. The office will provide the packet and walk you through anything that needs clarification.

  2. Custody orders (if applicable)

    Certified custody orders are required when applicable, so we can honor court-ordered pickup and communication preferences.

  3. Tuition fee paid

    Tuition must be paid according to your child's program and schedule. Ask the office for current rates for your age group.

  4. Supply fee paid

    A supply fee helps cover classroom materials and resources. Details are provided at enrollment.

  5. Interview with enrollment clerk or director

    You'll meet with our enrollment clerk or director to review policies, tour the center if you haven't already, and make sure the placement is a great fit.

Tuition & supply fees

Current tuition rates and supply fee amounts are provided by the office — we do not list dollar amounts on this website. Please call (432) 362-2785 or email aladdinscastle@aol.com for up-to-date pricing by age group and schedule.

What to expect

  • A warm welcome and clear communication about your child's classroom, teachers, and daily routine.
  • Guidance on what to bring (diapers, changes of clothes, labeled bottles, etc.) based on age group.
  • Preferred arrival by 10:00 a.m. so children can settle into the day's activities.
  • Hours: Monday–Friday, 7:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., year-round.

Ready to take the next step?

Call the office to check availability, request an enrollment packet, or schedule your interview and tour.
