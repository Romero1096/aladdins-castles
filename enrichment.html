Enrichment & after school

Movement, creativity, summer adventures, and reliable after-school care — extras that make the Castle special.

Schedule a tour / Enroll Call (432) 362-2785
Ages 3 – 5 · Weekly

Ballet

Weekly ballet for preschoolers ages 3–5. Children build balance, coordination, confidence, and a love of music and movement in a fun, encouraging class.

Optional enrichment — ask the office about current schedules and fees.

Ages 2 – 5 · Weekly

Stretch-n-Grow

Weekly fitness for ages 2–5 through Stretch-n-Grow. Kids stretch, move, and build healthy habits with age-appropriate exercise and playful energy.

Optional enrichment — contact us for availability.

After School Care & Pick Up

We provide after-school pickup from Odessa elementary campuses for kindergarten through 5th grade. Your child is welcomed back to the Castle with a snack, homework support, and supervised play until closing.

Snack + homework hour

A nutritious snack and dedicated time to get homework started — with staff nearby to encourage focus and good habits.

Elementary holidays

Free care on elementary holidays and in-service days for enrolled after-school families — so you're covered when school is out.

K – 5th grade

Reliable afternoon care that parents can count on. Call us to confirm pickup for your child's campus and to reserve a spot.

Campus list

We pick up from multiple Odessa elementary schools. Campus names and routes change with enrollment — please call the office at (432) 362-2785 for the current list and availability. We do not publish a fixed campus list on this site.

Summer for Schoolers

When school lets out, the adventure begins. Our summer program for kindergarten through 5th grade is themed, active, and fills early every year.

Themed weeks

Creative themes keep summer fresh — crafts, games, and activities that make each week feel special.

Field trips

Outings that kids look forward to — including pool days, bowling, Gattiland, and other local favorites.

Community service

Opportunities to give back and learn that kindness is part of growing up at the Castle.

Reserve early

Summer for Schoolers fills quickly. Call Mrs. April at (432) 362-2785 to ask about dates, themes, and enrollment.
